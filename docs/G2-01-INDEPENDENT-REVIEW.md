# G2-01 post-freeze causal review

Review scope: the locator author reviewed only the six public `g2-01-{healthy,observed,predictions,score,freeze,registration}.json` artifacts after prediction freeze. No private truth, plan, seed, runtime logs or oracle was read. This is a separate post-freeze causal review, not a fully independent replication of the experiment or truth evaluator.

**Decision:** retain the frozen scorer's `NO_GO_FOR_CURRENT_SIGNAL`; the registered raw Host-arrival comparator does not meet its engineering continuation criterion. The scientific conclusion about **eight isolated, independent primary-fault episodes is INCONCLUSIVE**, because recovery between episodes has not been demonstrated. G2 remains unpassed; do not tune this result into a pass or run further experiments under this pilot.

The [score](evidence/g2-01-score.json) reports 3/8 Top-1 matches, 0/8 injection UNKNOWN, and candidates in 6/8 fixed no-injection windows. Each evaluated window contains two matched collectives. The eight recorded sleeps span 20.060001–20.069952ms, supporting actual CPU sleep execution; this does not independently prove the downstream GPU delay magnitude or absence of other timing effects. The [registration](evidence/g2-01-registration.json) requires at least 7/8 matches and at most 1/8 such alarms. Both raw criteria fail.

The [predictions](evidence/g2-01-predictions.json) identify rank 2 at seq 17–18, then rank 1 continuously at seq 25–88 (64 consecutive collectives). The [score](evidence/g2-01-score.json) identifies rank 1 at both its own injection steps 8 and 12 and at later injections assigned to other ranks; its six no-injection-window candidates are also rank 1. This persistent Host phase difference is consistent with carryover or another continuing timing effect. Public artifacts do not establish its physical cause. Treating the six alarms as a clean isolated-health false-positive rate would overstate the evidence: these windows have no new scheduled injection, but have not been shown recovered from earlier perturbations. Likewise, report 3/8 as the observed pilot hit count, without independent-trial precision or population accuracy claims.

All 89 instances in each [healthy baseline](evidence/g2-01-healthy.json) and [observed input](evidence/g2-01-observed.json) are matched with four BOUNDED rank rows. The public input, prediction and registration file SHA256 values match the [freeze manifest](evidence/g2-01-freeze.json). These checks support preserved inputs and usable Host interval coverage, not end-to-end callback completeness, the private truth chronology, or GPU-kernel start semantics.

The useful negative finding is specific: strict raw Host-arrival lateness against a fixed healthy comparator can persist and cease to identify the newly delayed rank under this schedule. Further development should first obtain separately reviewed nonintrusive evidence of local progress and recovery, and a preregistered isolation criterion. That is a proposed next design for review, not authorization for another run, an altered threshold, or a Gate transition.
